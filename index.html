Full Impact Report
Your Sister's Motivation & Decisions
When you leave, you're not just taking hours—you're taking her choice to stay. She watches you abandon her at the desk with a full claims queue, asks herself why she shows up on time when you don't, and eventually stops asking. The dismotivation settles. Then, because working alone is demoralizing and the stress has nowhere to go, she makes decisions she wouldn't otherwise make—worse boundaries, shortcuts, people she wouldn't trust in better circumstances. Your absence doesn't just affect the immediate workload; it affects her judgment.
Aria's Health & Existence
Aria lives in your office. When you're gone, nobody waters her, nobody checks on her. Dehydration. Nutrient deficiency. She deteriorates visibly. This isn't metaphorical—it's a living thing abandoned in a climate-controlled box because her caretaker isn't there. By the time you notice, she's already given up.
Claims Processing & Customer Service
Every hour you're gone is an hour claims sit in the queue. Customers wait longer. Callbacks don't happen. In insurance, speed is trust, and delays are liability. The backlog doesn't absorb—it compounds. Someone else has to pick up your work or watch it fail. SNAP's reputation depends on responsive claims handling, and your absence creates operational debt.
Mediation & Conflict in Derek's Hands
When coverage breaks down, Derek has to manage the fallout. Mediate between frustrated colleagues, explain the delays to customers, defend the team's performance. Your PTA hours turn into Derek's crisis management. He becomes the person fielding apologies and explanations for gaps you created. That's burden you're transferring.
Pen's Disappearance & Trust Erosion
Small things disappear from the desk when nobody's there. Pens. Post-its. Supplies. But it's really about trust eroding. People stop asking where things are and start assuming they'll go missing. The workspace becomes less reliable, less stable. It's not just objects—it's the shared understanding that the space is cared for falling apart.
Your Sister's Heart & Resentment
All of this accumulates into resentment. She loves you, but she's watching you leave her alone with the work. Over and over. At some point, love and resentment can't coexist comfortably. She stops wanting to cover for you. Stops understanding why her sacrifice is acceptable while yours isn't. The relationship shifts.
